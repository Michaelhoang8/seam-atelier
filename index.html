Seam carving

RGB central-difference gradient magnitude estimates visual energy, with clamped edge neighbors. A dynamic program finds a minimum-cost connected path from top to bottom. Each removed seam triggers a fresh energy calculation. Height reduction transposes the problem. Ties favor the leftmost predecessor and endpoint.

Cyan protection adds 1,000,000 per pixel, more than the maximum gradient sum of a supported path. A seam avoids protected pixels whenever a zero-protection path exists. Protection may become unavoidable after repeated removals; the result reports every protected pixel removed.

This is backward-energy seam carving, not object recognition or globally optimal multi-seam removal. It can bend straight lines and distort content. Imports are flattened onto white and reduced to at most 256 × 256 working pixels. PNG, JPEG, and WebP files up to 8 MB and 16 megapixels are accepted; exported PNGs use the working resolution.

Paint with a pointer, or focus the original image, move with arrow keys, and press Space. Undo retains the last 20 mask edits. Sessions contain the complete working image, mask, and settings; they are limited to 2 MB. Images stay in the browser.
